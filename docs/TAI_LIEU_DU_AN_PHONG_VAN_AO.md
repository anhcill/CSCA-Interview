# Du an web phong thuc te ao luyen phong van du hoc Trung Quoc

## 1. Muc tieu san pham

Xay dung nen tang web giup hoc sinh, sinh vien luyen phong van du hoc Trung Quoc cho he Dai hoc va Thac si. He thong gia lap phong van that, dua tren thong tin ca nhan, ho so hoc tap, nganh, truong, loai hoc bong va ke hoach hoc tap cua tung nguoi dung.

San pham can giai quyet 4 nhu cau chinh:

- Nguoi dung duoc luyen phong van theo dung he, truong, nganh va hoc bong minh apply.
- AI co the tao them cau hoi dua tren profile va study plan cua nguoi dung.
- Nguoi dung nhan duoc goi y tra loi, phan tich cau hoi va danh gia logic cau tra loi.
- Admin co the quan ly kho cau hoi, mua tuyen sinh va ho so nguoi dung.

## 2. Doi tuong nguoi dung

### 2.1. Nguoi dung chinh

- Hoc sinh apply he Dai hoc tai Trung Quoc.
- Sinh vien apply he Thac si tai Trung Quoc.
- Ung vien apply hoc bong CSC, CIS, hoc bong tinh, hoc bong truong hoac tu tuc.

### 2.2. Admin/doi ngu van hanh

- Nguoi nhap va cap nhat kho cau hoi.
- Nguoi quan ly truong, nganh, hoc bong va mua tuyen sinh.
- Nguoi theo doi profile, lich su phong van va chat luong cau tra loi cua nguoi dung.

## 3. Tinh nang chinh

### 3.1. Dang ky va tao profile nguoi dung

Nguoi dung nhap cac thong tin:

- Ho ten.
- Tuoi.
- He apply: Dai hoc hoac Thac si.
- Truong apply.
- Nganh apply.
- Loai hoc bong.
- Chung chi ngoai ngu: HSK, HSKK, IELTS, TOEFL neu co.
- Thanh tich hoc tap.
- Nghien cuu khoa hoc, bai bao, de tai neu co.
- Hoat dong ngoai khoa.
- Kinh nghiem thuc tap/lam viec neu co.
- Ke hoach hoc tap.
- Dinh huong nghe nghiep sau khi tot nghiep.

### 3.2. Kho cau hoi phong van

Admin co the nhap va quan ly cau hoi theo cac tieu chi:

- He: Dai hoc/Thac si.
- Truong.
- Nganh.
- Loai hoc bong.
- Nhom cau hoi: ca nhan, hoc thuat, ke hoach hoc tap, hoc bong, dinh huong nghe nghiep, tieng Trung, tinh huong.
- Muc do: de, trung binh, kho.
- Ngon ngu: tieng Trung, tieng Viet, tieng Anh.
- Goi y logic tra loi.
- Tu khoa can co trong cau tra loi.
- Loi thuong gap.

Vi du nhom cau hoi:

- Gioi thieu ban than.
- Vi sao ban chon Trung Quoc?
- Vi sao ban chon truong nay?
- Vi sao ban chon nganh nay?
- Ban hieu gi ve nganh hoc minh apply?
- Ke hoach hoc tap trong 4 nam/2-3 nam la gi?
- Sau khi tot nghiep ban du dinh lam gi?
- Neu gap kho khan ve ngon ngu, ban se xu ly nhu the nao?
- De tai nghien cuu ban muon theo duoi la gi?

### 3.3. Phong phong van ao

Giao dien can tao cam giac nhu mot buoi phong van that:

- Man hinh phong van co avatar nguoi phong van.
- Khung hoi thoai hien cau hoi va cau tra loi.
- Nut bat dau, tam dung, tra loi, ket thuc phong van.
- Hien thi ngon ngu phong van dang su dung.
- Co the chon che do phong van:
  - Luyen tap co goi y.
  - Phong van thu nghiem khong goi y.
  - Phong van cham diem.
- Co lich su cau hoi da hoi.
- Co dong ho dem thoi gian tra loi.
- Co ket qua sau buoi phong van.

### 3.4. AI gia lap phong van

AI can xu ly 3 nguon du lieu:

- Profile nguoi dung.
- Ke hoach hoc tap cua nguoi dung.
- Kho cau hoi cua he thong.

AI se thuc hien:

- Chon cau hoi phu hop voi he, truong, nganh va hoc bong.
- Tao them cau hoi moi dua tren profile va study plan.
- Dieu chinh do kho theo trinh do cua nguoi dung.
- Phan tich cau hoi de nguoi dung hieu y do cua ban phong van.
- Goi y cau tra loi theo cau truc logic.
- Danh gia cau tra loi cua nguoi dung.
- Phat hien cau tra loi thieu y, lan man, sai trong tam hoac chua thuyet phuc.

### 3.5. Cham diem va phan tich cau tra loi

Sau moi cau tra loi, he thong co the cham theo cac tieu chi:

- Do lien quan voi cau hoi.
- Do ro rang cua cau tra loi.
- Logic trinh bay.
- Muc do phu hop voi truong/nganh/hoc bong.
- Do thuyet phuc cua ly do apply.
- Nang luc ngon ngu.
- Diem manh can phat huy.
- Diem yeu can sua.
- Goi y phien ban tra loi tot hon.

Ket qua cuoi buoi phong van:

- Tong diem.
- Danh sach cau hoi da hoi.
- Cau tra loi cua nguoi dung.
- Nhan xet tung cau.
- Cac loi lap lai nhieu lan.
- De xuat noi dung can luyen tiep.

### 3.6. Giong noi

He thong ho tro:

- Giong AI text-to-speech.
- Giong nguoi that duoc thu am san.
- Ngon ngu: tieng Trung, tieng Viet, tieng Anh.
- Nguoi dung co the nghe cau hoi bang audio.
- Nguoi dung co the tra loi bang text trong MVP, sau do nang cap tra loi bang voice.

Huong phat trien nang cao:

- Speech-to-text de nguoi dung tra loi bang giong noi.
- Phan tich phat am tieng Trung.
- Danh gia do troi chay va toc do noi.

## 4. Trang admin

Admin can co cac module:

### 4.1. Quan ly nguoi dung

- Xem danh sach nguoi dung.
- Xem profile chi tiet.
- Xem lich su phong van.
- Xem diem va bao cao luyen tap.
- Khoa/mo khoa tai khoan neu can.

### 4.2. Quan ly kho cau hoi

- Them, sua, xoa cau hoi.
- Gan cau hoi theo he, truong, nganh, hoc bong.
- Gan ngon ngu va muc do.
- Import cau hoi bang file Excel/CSV.
- Tim kiem va loc cau hoi.

### 4.3. Quan ly truong, nganh, hoc bong

- Them truong.
- Them nganh.
- Them loai hoc bong.
- Cap nhat du lieu theo mua tuyen sinh.

### 4.4. Quan ly noi dung audio

- Upload file thu am cau hoi.
- Gan audio voi cau hoi.
- Chon giong AI mac dinh theo ngon ngu.

## 5. Cong nghe de xuat

### 5.1. Frontend

De xuat:

- Next.js hoac React.
- TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS.
- shadcn/ui hoac Ant Design cho dashboard/admin.
- Web Speech API hoac SDK speech rieng neu can thu am/giong noi tren trinh duyet.

Ly do:

- Phu hop de lam web app hien dai.
- De chia role nguoi dung/admin.
- De phat trien giao dien phong van ao, dashboard va form nhap lieu.

### 5.2. Backend

De xuat:

- NestJS hoac Express.js voi TypeScript.
- REST API cho ban dau.
- WebSocket neu can phong van realtime hon.
- Job queue cho cac tac vu AI/audio ton thoi gian.

Ly do:

- De to chuc code theo module.
- Phu hop voi he thong co user, admin, AI, voice, database.
- De mo rong khi co nhieu nguoi dung.

### 5.3. Database

De xuat:

- PostgreSQL lam database chinh.
- Prisma ORM de quan ly schema va query.
- Redis cho cache, session hoac queue neu can.

Ly do:

- Du lieu co quan he ro rang: user, profile, cau hoi, truong, nganh, hoc bong, lich su phong van.
- PostgreSQL on dinh va de mo rong.

### 5.4. AI

De xuat:

- Dung API AI language model de tao cau hoi, phan tich study plan va danh gia cau tra loi.
- Thiet ke lop `AI Provider` rieng de sau nay co the doi nha cung cap AI.
- Dung prompt template rieng cho tung tac vu:
  - Tao cau hoi phong van.
  - Phan tich ke hoach hoc tap.
  - Goi y cau tra loi.
  - Cham diem cau tra loi.
  - Tao bao cao sau phong van.

Can luu y:

- Khong dua toan bo du lieu nguoi dung vao prompt neu khong can.
- Can co co che an danh/thiet lap bao mat thong tin ca nhan.
- Ket qua AI nen duoc luu lai de nguoi dung xem lich su.

### 5.5. Voice

Text-to-speech:

- Dung AI TTS de doc cau hoi bang tieng Trung, tieng Viet, tieng Anh.
- Ho tro upload file audio thu am that.

Speech-to-text:

- Giai doan MVP co the cho nguoi dung nhap text.
- Giai doan sau them ghi am va chuyen giong noi thanh van ban.

### 5.6. Luu tru file

De xuat:

- S3-compatible storage, Cloudflare R2, AWS S3 hoac Supabase Storage.
- Luu file audio, avatar, tai lieu profile neu co.

### 5.7. Xac thuc va phan quyen

De xuat:

- Email/password hoac OTP.
- JWT/session authentication.
- Role-based access control:
  - User.
  - Admin.
  - Super admin.

### 5.8. Hosting

Phuong an don gian:

- Frontend: Vercel.
- Backend: Render, Railway, Fly.io hoac VPS.
- Database: Supabase, Neon, Railway PostgreSQL.
- File storage: Cloudflare R2 hoac Supabase Storage.

Phuong an chuyen nghiep hon:

- Docker.
- VPS rieng.
- PostgreSQL managed.
- CI/CD bang GitHub Actions.

## 6. Kien truc tong quan

```text
Nguoi dung/Admin
      |
      v
Frontend Web App
      |
      v
Backend API
      |
      +--> PostgreSQL: user, profile, question bank, interview session
      |
      +--> File Storage: audio, avatar, uploaded files
      |
      +--> AI Service: tao cau hoi, phan tich, cham diem
      |
      +--> Voice Service: text-to-speech, speech-to-text
```

## 7. Luong xu ly phong van

1. Nguoi dung tao profile.
2. Nguoi dung nhap study plan.
3. He thong loc cau hoi tu kho du lieu theo he, truong, nganh, hoc bong.
4. AI doc profile va study plan de tao them cau hoi ca nhan hoa.
5. He thong tao mot interview session.
6. Avatar/AI interviewer lan luot hoi cau hoi.
7. Nguoi dung tra loi bang text hoac voice.
8. AI phan tich cau tra loi.
9. He thong luu cau hoi, cau tra loi, diem va nhan xet.
10. Ket thuc buoi phong van, he thong tao bao cao tong ket.

## 9. Prompt AI can co

### 9.1. Prompt tao cau hoi

Muc tieu:

- Tao cau hoi dua tren profile, study plan, nganh, truong va hoc bong.
- Khong tao cau hoi qua xa voi thong tin nguoi dung.
- Chia cau hoi theo muc do va nhom noi dung.

### 9.2. Prompt phan tich cau hoi

Muc tieu:

- Giai thich cau hoi dang muon kiem tra dieu gi.
- Neu cac y nen co trong cau tra loi.
- Canh bao loi tra loi thuong gap.

### 9.3. Prompt cham diem cau tra loi

Muc tieu:

- Cham diem theo rubric.
- Giai thich ly do cham diem.
- Goi y cau tra loi tot hon.
- Neu diem manh va diem yeu.

## 10. Bao mat va quyen rieng tu

- Ma hoa mat khau bang bcrypt/argon2.
- Phan quyen ro giua user va admin.
- Chi admin duoc sua kho cau hoi.
- Khong hien thi profile nguoi dung cho nguoi khac.
- Ghi log cac thao tac quan trong cua admin.
- Co chinh sach xoa tai khoan va xoa du lieu ca nhan.
- Han che gui du lieu nhay cam sang AI neu khong can thiet.

## 11. MVP de xuat

Nen lam ban dau theo pham vi gon:

### Giai doan 1

- Dang ky/dang nhap.
- Tao profile nguoi dung.
- Admin them/sua/xoa cau hoi.
- Loc cau hoi theo he, nganh, hoc bong.
- Phong van ao bang text.
- AI tao them cau hoi dua tren study plan.
- AI goi y va cham diem cau tra loi.
- Luu lich su phong van.

### Giai doan 2

- Avatar phong van.
- Text-to-speech doc cau hoi.
- Upload audio thu am that.
- Bao cao chi tiet sau phong van.
- Import cau hoi bang Excel/CSV.

### Giai doan 3

- Speech-to-text de tra loi bang giong noi.
- Phan tich phat am tieng Trung.
- Goi y study plan tot hon.
- Goi y chien luoc phong van theo tung truong/nganh.
- Thanh toan goi luyen tap neu muon thuong mai hoa.

## 12. Rui ro va cach xu ly

### 12.1. Cau hoi AI khong dung trong tam

Cach xu ly:

- Luon ket hop AI voi kho cau hoi do admin kiem soat.
- Cau hoi AI tao ra nen co tag va co the duoc admin duyet.
- Dung prompt gioi han theo profile nguoi dung.

### 12.2. Du lieu phong van thay doi theo mua tuyen sinh

Cach xu ly:

- Admin co module cap nhat cau hoi theo nam/mua tuyen sinh.
- Cau hoi co truong `season` hoac `admission_year`.

### 12.3. Chi phi AI va voice cao

Cach xu ly:

- Cache ket qua phan tich study plan.
- Gioi han so lan luyen tap theo goi tai khoan.
- Uu tien text trong MVP, voice dua vao giai doan sau.

### 12.4. Nguoi dung nhap profile qua ngan

Cach xu ly:

- Form profile can co goi y truong bat buoc.
- AI dat cau hoi bo sung neu profile thieu thong tin.

## 13. Tieu chi hoan thanh san pham ban dau

- User tao duoc profile day du.
- Admin quan ly duoc kho cau hoi.
- He thong tao duoc mot buoi phong van theo profile.
- AI hoi duoc cau hoi ca nhan hoa.
- User tra loi va nhan duoc nhan xet.
- He thong luu lai lich su phong van.
- Admin xem duoc thong tin nguoi dung va ket qua phong van.

## 14. Huong mo rong kinh doanh

- Goi mien phi: so luot phong van gioi han.
- Goi tra phi theo thang.
- Goi phong van theo truong/nganh/hoc bong cu the.
- Goi cham sua study plan.
- Goi luyen phat am tieng Trung.
- Dashboard cho trung tam tu van du hoc.
